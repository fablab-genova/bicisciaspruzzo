# Air/water tank

The air/water tank is a pressurized vessel that stores the liquid to be sprayed.

## Purposes

The purpose of this module is to store the liquid to be sprayed and the pressure needed to spray it.

## Features

### mandatory

- max pressure: at least 8 bar
- volume: at least 1 liter
- input port for the compressed air with no-return valve or check valve
- input port for the liquid
- output port for the pressurized liquid
- format of the output port compatible with the pressure regulator

### nice to have

- max pressure: at least 10 bar
- volume: at least 20 liters
- discharge port for releasing the pressure in case of emergency

## State of the art

We did some tests with an helium bottle and a pressure regulator. The results were ok, but the connections are too limited. We need to find a solution that is more flexible and easy to use. We also need to find a solution that is more compact and easier to transport.

We put our hands on a beer keg that has more volume and has an integrated I/O port
